Creating corporate training courses with the help of AI

We turn webinars, regulations and instructions into ready-made training courses — quickly, accurately and with the participation of your experts.

THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

Developing corporate training is hundreds of hours of manual work: transcription, structuring, writing texts, creating presentations and tests. Instructional designers and experts spend time on routine instead of pedagogical design and quality control.

AI automates the preparation of educational materials, leaving people with the most important part — expertise and meaningful decisions.

HOW IT WORKS

Scenario 1. From a webinar recording — to a training course

Source material: an employee's presentation, an expert's webinar, a training session or a master class.

  1. The system transcribes the recording.
  2. AI identifies topics and semantic fragments.
  3. The content is transformed into a course structure based on the principles of instructional design.
  4. Learning goals and objectives are defined; modules and chapters are formed.
  5. Lesson texts are created; illustrations are selected and refined.

Result: the learner easily finds the necessary topics, returns to individual explanations and masters the material in small portions.

Scenario 2. From a document — to educational material

Source material: internal regulations, instructions, normative documents, methodological materials.

Example: updated occupational safety rules are transformed into a short training module that explains the main requirements and the employee's course of action.

AI helps to:

  • highlight key provisions;
  • organize the content;
  • prepare clear explanations.

Before the material is used, a subject-matter expert verifies the accuracy of the wording and compliance with the source documents.

Result: existing documentation becomes the basis for training, and preparing materials when procedures change takes less time.

WHAT YOU GET
  • a course syllabus with learning goals and objectives;
  • structured text materials, modules and chapters;
  • presentations and illustrative materials;
  • combinations of text and video content for different learning formats;
  • interactive elements: surveys, tests, flip cards and other training blocks (in development);
  • course packaging into a SCORM package for loading into a compatible LMS (in development).

The content undergoes methodological and expert review. Automation accelerates preparation while preserving the participation of specialists in making meaningful decisions.

APPLICATION EXPERIENCE

Manufacturing of multimedia equipment (Russia)

The source of materials is an archive of public webinars for partners. Based on it, courses for employees were prepared. The internal training base contains more than 30 ready-made courses created using the technology.

Pharmacology and medicine (consulting)

The source materials are educational webinars for doctors lasting 3–4 hours. The content was transformed into training products combining video, text and interactive game elements. The main content part is preserved; learners have an additional way to study the material alongside watching the full recording.
