Unique preparation technology

Vegetables, fruits, berries and grains are processed together with their skins and seeds. This greatly increases the value of the final product in terms of the content of biologically active substances.

The use of critically low temperatures during preparation allows the shelf life to be increased up to 18 months without preservatives.

Synthetic substances are NOT used throughout the entire production cycle.

The storage temperature range is unlimited, which allows the product to be used both in polar cold conditions and in very hot climatic zones.

Unique cryogenic grinding technology

The products are prepared in the form of hermetically sealed dry vitamin and mineral powders for the quick preparation of drinks with high biological activity.

Plant raw materials are used for preparation — traditionally eaten and grown in ecologically clean regions of Russia and the CIS.

A patented (21 patents) technology of critically low temperatures and special grinding is used, maximally preserving biologically active substances in the raw material and increasing their bioavailability.

The concentration of biologically active substances increases by 8–12 times, and the degree of absorption reaches 100%.

The color, taste and aroma of the original raw materials are fully preserved in the mixtures.
